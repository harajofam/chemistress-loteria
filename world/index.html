Before you enter

A quick word before you enter 💜

  • This is a living 3D world that runs in your browser. It needs your internet connection — and the display name and character you create, which are kept on your device.
  • If you tap “Look around with your phone” or “Viewer Mode,” your browser will ask to use your phone's motion sensor. That's the only device permission this world ever requests — it just lets the view follow your hand, and nothing is recorded.
  • Films and sounds play through your browser, like any video.
  • We never ask for your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or files — and there are no trackers in these rooms.
  • If your browser ever shows a warning about “accessing other apps and services,” tap Block — this world doesn't need that. (A leftover test address in our older game page caused that warning; it's been removed.)
  • While you're inside, our server holds your display name, character look, seat and game moves, your garden plant's growth, and any pen-pal note you post — so the community can see you and play with you. When members-only entry begins, the door also records your account ID and the time you confirmed you're 18 or older. No birthdate is ever stored.
